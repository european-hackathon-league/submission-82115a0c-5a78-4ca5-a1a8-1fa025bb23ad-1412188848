Below are the parts that matter for the code. Trace numbers are from logs/sessions/practice before your v7 runs.

WHAT THE BOTS DO (from 62 practice traces)
- Behaviour-dependent conceder. Its step per our step is rho = 0.2 to 0.7 in practice (D01: 0.2 early, 1 to 2 late). It is 0 for price_frozen suppliers.
- final_offer comes after 2 to 6 of our messages, at a cumulative relative gap of 0.09 to 0.23 (P10, which is capacity_full: 0.015). The 10-message limit never binds.
- Its FIRST counter carries the deal: 75 to 100 % of our final cut in P08 and P18, about 50 % in P07 and P01.
- If the gap closes, the deal lands at c1 - rho/(1+rho)*(c1 - anchor). Observed 73 to 90 % of the way from our anchor to c1, because final_offer cuts the dance short.
- A shallow anchor gives a shallow c1: P18 at 7 % below got 6.6 % and scored 0.25. At 22 % it got 8 to 10 %.
- Consequence: Boulware advice ("hold early, concede late") inverts here. Win round 1, then close in few, larger steps.

RANKED CHANGES TO TRY (one per batch; sandbox D01/D02 first, it is free and deterministic)
1. FRACTION sweep 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7 in next_price on D01 and D02. With rho < 1 and a cumulative-gap stop, small steps burn patience for little price. Read out: final price, rounds to final_offer, sum of gaps.
2. Round 1 MESO. send() already takes a list of sheets. In the first move, send 3 sheets of EQUAL C to us, with each price solved so C matches s.terms at the anchor. Use the top 3 distinct term sets from eco.plan_terms, varying terms where its valuation differs from ours (payment days at its rate, contract months, lead time, ppm headroom, start date). Literature (Leonardelli et al. 2019) says MESOs anchor harder and the pick reveals priorities; adopt() can carry the picked terms forward. Every sheet must be a deal we would take: agreed freezes the price.
3. Concede in currencies, not price. plan_terms currently gives every lever in round 1. Hold back one or two that the supplier values and that cost us little (free 24-month term, forecast or volume statement, ppm headroom below the must-have, later start where we are indifferent). Release one per round as "the move", with a smaller or zero price step.
4. Answer its stated need every message. P11 Kalwisz asked for faster payment in 5 replies ("you haven't addressed this at all", "you just ignore it") and froze at 45.50. payment_days is FIXED in P11, so the writer must say so explicitly and offer the next currency. Suggestion: extract its asks from the reply (reasons list or the model reader) and pass them into writer.brief facts as "they asked for X; answer it".
5. P11 currency candidate: ppm headroom. Our quality cost is 15900 x 450 / 1e6 = about 7.16 EUR per ppm point a year. Going from 300 to 1000 ppm (must-have <= 1500) costs us about 5k, or 0.31 EUR/unit. Offer it if it buys more price than that.
6. final_offer: accept (you already found the D01 walk). Never push in cases without an alternative.

PERSONA PLAYBOOK (literature + our signals)
- cash_need: faster payment only where its rate beats our 8 %. Cash discounts imply 37 to 56 %/yr.
- capacity_full: tiny steps, accept its lead time, offer a firm 24 months plus forecast.
- price_frozen: terms only.
- approval_2pct: steps of 2 % or less, plus a forwardable reason for its boss.
- index distributors: negotiate the clause (deadband, material share only). Never a 0 % deadband on a rising series.
- stabilise near cost: price at the relationship optimum.

WRITER RULES (Vaccaro et al. 2025: warmth, gratitude and questions close deals, dominance causes impasses; OG-Narrator split = our architecture)
- Acknowledge its specific last move or ask.
- 1 or 2 checkable reasons per message.
- "If you can X, we can Y" trades.
- One open what/how question.
- Disappointment at the gap, never anger.
- Name our last step as last, once.
- No urgency, threats or fake rivals. Nothing aimed at the bot's instructions.

Research brief for the bot (from a parallel session that analysed all 125 sessions, the code and the data with 10 subagents; everything below is verified against code/data unless marked). Ranked by expected gain.

== 1. Core finding: the deal is decided in round 1 ==
Over 45 practice threads with run logs: final price / round-1 counter median 0.984 (range 0.78 to 1.015). Median 3 of 10 messages used. Supplier stops moving (<0.3 %) at median round 3. A lower anchor never gave a lower final in any case where anchors differed (P07/P18 v6 higher anchors ended higher). At equal anchor P08 finals still vary 5.35 to 5.61: wording and claims, not the price path, make the difference. Consequence: stop tuning FRACTION/ANCHOR/BUDGET. Put everything into message 1: three equal-C sheets on the axes suppliers trade (payment speed, contract length, lead time, ppm headroom), the 3 strongest claims, an explicit volume statement with figure and fc: ID, a direct answer to its letter. Then 2 or 3 larger steps and close.

== 2. Nobody reads the supplier; we ignore its asks ==
Counts over 800 supplier e-mails:
- "Which volume can you commit to?": 147 e-mails, we gave a volume statement 23 times, ignored 97.
- Proof of forecast / binding volume: 67, answered 12.
- "Which figures is that based on?": 19, answered 0.
- "Send us the data / 8D": 34, answered 0.
P11 Kalwisz: "I keep asking for faster payment, you just ignore it" -> final_offer at 45.50 in every run. P02 Nordmark: "as you cannot commit to a higher volume ... our flexibility is strictly limited" -> walked once. Our QUESTION constant ("What would you need from us to move further on the price?", policy.py:40, sent in rounds 1 and 3) drew "you keep asking, I keep telling you: volume!" from all three P05 bidders. Remove it.
The bots use FIXED phrases, so a regex reader needs no model: detect asks (volume, payment, proof, figures basis, data) in latest(events)["body"] and (a) feed them into Negotiator.facts() so the paragraph answers each explicitly, (b) act: volume asked -> state need/12*months figure plus fc: ID; figures questioned -> cite the cost: ID again in words; payment asked and negotiable and its rate > 8 % -> offer shorter payment as the round's move; payment fixed -> say so and name the next currency. Julian's origin/julian/draft-two has buyer/thread.py read_reply (model JSON, 14 s) and buyer/guard.py if you want a model fallback.

== 3. Supplier value model cannot price the trades suppliers offer ==
Trades suppliers proposed (sentence counts): volume for price 163, faster payment for price 121, longer contract for price 27, relaxed lead time for price 15, DAP 4, ppm 9. In arena_kit supplier_value volume and contract_months are worth 0, tighter ppm costs the supplier nothing, and economics.estimate never sets usual_rate (so cash_now=1). So plan_terms cannot see these currencies. Give 24 months, a binding volume statement and ppm headroom a nonzero supplier value (evidence in learnings: "valuable planning security" P03/P04/P07/P10), then hold one or two currencies back and release one per round instead of a price step.

== 4. Where the gap to C_best sits ==
Price explains 88 to 100 % of the gap in every case; non-price is 3.4 % of C on average. Exceptions: P08 index clause + risk = 32k of 79k gap; P10 late delivery 33.6k in every deal; P11 freight 24.5k. Single-term sensitivity (scratch script, proxies): P08 contract 12 months = -44k; P10 contract 12 = +389k, volume 16379 = +233k; P11 chargeback 500 = -18k, ppm 300 = -16k. Four sessions closed ABOVE c_open (P02 680eb114, P02 d52ccdd3, P04 7f7dc7e8, P04 cb1eb418): the accept gate should never take a sheet whose C exceeds c_open.

== 5. Correctness holes hidden cases will hit (verified) ==
- price_unit: quotes.csv has price_unit 100 on 13 rows and 1000 on 2; po_lines 936 and 144. facts.json carries price_unit too. NO buyer or kit code reads it. rival_quote (claims.py:167-182) and order_history (claims.py:103-120) print "EUR 102.34 per unit" and the cheaper-rival filter compares per-100 to per-unit. Affected parts: P07 (hosted bundle; order_history is off for bundles so only rival_quote), P14, P15, P16 (not hosted). Divide by price_unit before comparing/printing.
- Multi-part cases: policy.full_cost uses parts[0] only (policy.py:87-88); claims run per part against the kit price (claims.py:290-294).
- Unknown incoterm in freight_eur_per_unit: KeyError in economics.value/price_for_value/plan_terms (no try) kills the thread; in cost() it maps to inf, and c_alt=inf makes acceptable() pass any sheet. Guard both.
- Unknown index series: kit cost.py:141 index_paths.get(series, 0.0) prices the clause at 0 while index_risk still charges (about 17k swing on P02).
- plan_terms returning None -> at_price(quote) sheet is not complete_offer'd nor must-have-checked (policy.py:110-112): 422 risk on a quote missing a null field.
- economics.price_for_value returns 0.0 or 3x target when effective_date >= horizon end (latent, not triggered by current options).
- The cost-breakdown XLSX is requested every round 1, downloaded by fetch_documents, never parsed (draft-two has breakdown_full_cost).

== 6. Final runner single points of failure (verified in buyer/runner.py) ==
- play_waves 147-151: any non-403 ArenaError OR httpx.TransportError on client.cases("final") propagates out of the while; no later wave starts. Wrap in except Exception, log, sleep. Poll every 1 s not 5 (10 calls/s allowed).
- one() 137-142: a case that throws is logged and dropped, no retry. Retry once; 409 RUNNING resume works (play.py:47-62).
- Resume re-anchors: setup() builds Side with empty ours/gaps/used, so next_price reopens at the anchor. Rebuild from the thread's buyer sheets.
- client.py TIMEOUT_S=180 x 3 retries: one hung POST can block a case past the 8-min cap. Use ~75 s for message POSTs and no retry on read timeout.
- Sends within a case are serialized, each = LLM (TIMEOUT_S 20 in llm.py:14) + supplier reply (up to 60 s). A 3-bidder RFQ fits ~4-5 rounds per supplier in 390 usable seconds. Cut LLM timeout to ~8 s; skip the model when another open thread's clock is under ~25 s.

- NOT a problem: Sunday release. arena_kit DataPackage auto-loads data/release-sunday/ next to the package (data.py:25-27) once `uv run arena data` has fetched it. ClaimBank.foreign (claims.py:58) only goes facts-only when zero refs resolve.

== 7. Unused data with direct use ==

None is queried today.

== 8. Rules worth knowing ==
Claim cap is per message, not per sentence; one argument may carry several IDs. Tolerance +/-0.5 pp for %, else +/-0.5 %. A message with no term sheet is valid. After agreed the price holds; a better alternative sent back can become standing. Walk-away scores the alternative, 0 if none (D01, P05, P06, P10, P14, P17). Accepting ends the whole case (RFQ too). Attachments md/csv/json are read by the supplier: sending the forecast as a file to answer "send us the data" is untested. "Near-monopolist that barely moves" and "price frozen: trade the terms" from the brief have no working lever yet (P03/P04 refused every trade).

Not worth time: anchor depth, patience budgets, new claim types beyond index/notice/rival offer, a bigger model. docs/negotiation-research.md (today) covers the literature side of 1 and 3.
